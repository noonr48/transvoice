# TransVoice spoken Coach — thumb-zone refinement

- **Surface:** the single-screen, voice-only Coach lesson in the Android WebView served from `/app` over the tailnet gateway.
- **Audience:** a learner who opens the phone to practise aloud and should be able to begin with one comfortable thumb press.
- **Tone:** calm, direct, private, and immediate; a live vocal lesson, never a messaging product.
- **Brand source:** the existing graphite room, warm amber action, Manrope type, and named target-voice preset.
- **Scale:** portrait phone first, anchored to the dynamic viewport and safe-area insets without document scrolling.
- **Constraints:** exactly two persistent controls; one instructional text canvas; one compact non-interactive activity line while the lesson is running; ≥44px targets; no chat, playback, helper captions, forced warm-up, or coach-directed stop.
- **Treatment:** utilitarian restyle — refine one existing Coach surface without adding a screen, flow, or control.
- **Intent-feel:** starting or ending practice should feel physically immediate: the action lands in the natural thumb zone one-third up from the bottom and says only `Start` or `End`; once capture is genuinely armed, `Ready — speak now` makes the invitation unmistakable without exposing pipeline detail.

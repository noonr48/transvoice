## Dependency Order

S1 proves the optional model boundary and S2 proves the server endpoint state without touching the live server. S3 preserves one ASR/session mutation owner. S4 forges the authenticated crossing; S5 advertises it only after attachment. S6 hardens rollback independently. S7 derives a minimal visible state from facts. S8 watches the new seams. S9 declares stable config before S10 installs anything. S11 builds after tests; S12 is the live gateway mutation. S13 is read-only device/product-law evidence and S14 is its review gate. Owner feedback reopened the output seam as S15: TTS service deploys before the rebuilt/restarted gateway, followed by silent phone-route proof. No step requires a later step to compile or roll back.

## Done When

- Every measurable criterion in `scope.md` has a receipt under `design/architecture/verify/` or `design/frontend/verify/`.
- A real human fixture with a 1.2-second deliberate pause crosses the deployed gateway without premature finalization; physical-phone careful/stutter corpus validation remains an explicit residual rather than an inferred claim.
- A completed turn reaches GPU ASR without the phone MediaRecorder/base64 path and produces one coach response.
- The four-state status line is visible, accessible, no-scroll, and adds no control or messaging semantics.
- Detector loss or uncertainty is observable and safely falls back at exactly 4.5 seconds; no semantic decision is accepted before the exact 1.8-second candidate boundary.
- The selected preset remains the tutor voice; the only persistent controls remain preset + `Start`/`End`, with the latter at `66.667dvh`.
- Selected-reference audio is conditioning-only, the generated words match target text, the normal tutor pace is objectively near 132 WPM, and automated transport gates reject every known partial-delivery path. Complete phone-speaker delivery, perceived pace/emphasis, and preset identity remain a human acceptance gate.
- Deep-review closes with no unowned seam or unproven load-bearing witness.

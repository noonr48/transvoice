## WHY THIS MATTERS

The B1-B11 research gives the app **ingredients** (every voice quality decomposed biomechanically, sound-tools, bridging techniques, safety, singing, non-speech vocalizations, surgery rehab). What was missing is the **recipe** — the actual learner-facing progression that turns an absolute beginner into someone who can use a feminine voice in real life.

This branch synthesizes B6's 5-phase timeline + B7's layer model + B8a-c's sound-tools + B8d's Master Bridge Protocol into:
1. A first-session onboarding flow (what the learner experiences on day one)
2. A 24-week curriculum scaffold with concrete daily sessions
3. Assessment checkpoints between phases
4. Failure-mode recovery paths

## SECTION 1 — ONBOARDING (first session, ~15-20 min)

### What the learner sees/hears on first launch

**Step 1 — Welcome & orientation (2 min, mostly video/audio)**
- Brief, warm welcome — this is hard emotional work, you belong here
- Set expectation: this is a NEUROMUSCULAR RELEARNING process, like learning to write with your non-dominant hand. Months, not days.
- Honest framing: ~80% of consistent practicers reach female-typical pitch in 6-12 months. Many never need surgery. Daily 15-30 min practice is required.
- "What success looks like": at 1 month (body awareness + can find one or two qualities); at 3 months (qualities starting to combine, occasional use in safe contexts); at 6 months (conversational use with trusted people); at 12 months (largely automatic).

**Step 2 — Goal & context check (3 min, interactive)**
- "What's your goal?" — Passing? Singing? Phone calls only? Work voice? All of the above? (drives curriculum weighting)
- "Have you done voice training before?" — Yes (skip ahead, assess) / No (start at week 1) / Some YouTube (start at week 1 with body awareness, may move faster)
- "Are you on HRT?" — note: estrogen does NOT raise voice pitch after testosterone puberty (B6 fact)
- "Are you considering surgery?" — note for later; default curriculum is therapy-first
- "Do you have any voice conditions?" — nodules, MTD, reflux, asthma, hearing loss, neurodivergence (triggers B14 content)
- "How much time can you commit daily?" — 10 min / 15-20 min / 30+ min (drives session length)

**Step 3 — Body awareness baseline (5 min, guided audio)**
- "Stand or sit tall. Feet grounded. Knees soft. Shoulders dropped."
- Body scan from B7 §1.4 (10-second version)
- 3 easy breaths into lower belly
- "Hum softly on a comfortable note. Where do you feel the buzz?" (collects self-rated buzz location: chest / throat / face / teeth / lips)
- "Say 'ahhhh' on a comfortable note. Where does the buzz go?"
- "Make an 'sssss' sound. Is the hiss forward in your teeth or back in your throat?" (collects /s/ placement)
- This baseline recording becomes the user's "Day 0" snapshot to compare against later

**Step 4 — First tiny win (5 min)**
- Teach ONE thing the learner can do in 60 seconds that produces a noticeable shift
- Best first-win: the **forward buzz** via /m/ hum (B8a — easiest sound-tool, sustainable, immediate face buzz)
- "Close your lips. Hum softly like 'mmm.' Feel the buzz in your lips and face."
- "Now hum slightly higher. Notice the buzz moves more forward and up into your face."
- "Now smile slightly while you hum. The buzz gets brighter."
- The learner has now FELT the forward-buzz sensation — the foundation quality for everything else

**Step 5 — Lesson 1 assignment (3 min)**
- Daily practice for week 1: 10 min
- The body scan (1 min) + hum-on-comfortable-note exploration (5 min) + body relaxation (4 min)
- "Tomorrow we'll add the front-throat stretch. Today, just feel the buzz."
- Schedule reminder, app daily push, etc.

**Step 6 — Setting expectations for the journey (2 min)**
- The 5 phases overview (body → isolation → integration → conversation → consolidation)
- "Don't skip ahead. Plateaus are normal. Rest days are necessary. Voice work should NEVER hurt."
- The safety stop-list (B6 §9): sharp pain, persistent ache, voice loss, tightness that won't release, etc. — STOP, rest 24-48 hr, see ENT if >2 weeks
- "Welcome. We're glad you're here."

## SECTION 2 — THE 5-PHASE CURRICULUM (24 weeks total at standard pace)

### Phase 1 — Body Awareness (Weeks 1-2)

**Daily session: 10-15 min**

**Goals:**
- Feel the breath go low (belly + ribs)
- Feel the throat open and release
- Feel buzz location and learn to move it (chest → face)
- Build daily practice habit

**Daily session structure (10 min):**
1. Body scan (1 min)
2. Easy breath into lower belly ×5 (1 min)
3. Hum on comfortable note, feel buzz in lips/face (3 min)
4. Sigh on "ah" ×3 (1 min)
5. Lip trill (motor-boat) ×5 (2 min)
6. Body release: jaw, tongue, shoulders (2 min)

**No performance goals yet.** Just feel.

**Assessment checkpoint (end of week 2):**
- Can the learner consistently feel breath in lower belly? (Y/N)
- Can they feel the buzz move from chest to face on a hum? (Y/N)
- Is the throat staying soft during hums? (Y/N)
- Have they established a daily practice habit (5+ days/week)?

If "no" on any → repeat Phase 1 for another week.

### Phase 2 — Single Quality Isolation (Weeks 3-8)

**Daily session: 15-20 min**

**Goals:** Master each of the 5 voice qualities (B7 §2) in isolation, ONE AT A TIME, using sound-tools (B8a-c) as wedges.

**The quality sequence (from B7 §3.3):**
1. Front-throat stretch (higher voice) — weeks 3-4
2. Buzz moving forward (mouth shape) — weeks 5-6 (BIGGEST perceptual shift)
3. Light voice (less chest rumble) — weeks 7-8

(Save breathy edge and sentence melody for Phase 3 — they're additive, not foundational)

**Daily session structure (15-20 min):**
1. Phase 1 warmup (5 min) — body scan, breath, hum, lip trill
2. Quality of the fortnight — sound-tool wedge + drill (8-10 min)
   - Week 3-4: Sirens + ng-glide for voice-box stretch
   - Week 5-6: /s/ + /i/ + /m/ for forward buzz
   - Week 7-8: Sigh + lip trill + almost-laugh for light voice
3. Sustain single sound or syllable in new posture (3 min)
4. Cooldown: gentle sighs, body release (2 min)

**Assessment checkpoint (end of week 8):**
- Can the learner produce the front-throat stretch without squeezing extrinsic muscles? (sensation check)
- Can they sustain forward buzz on /m/ for 10 sec? 
- Can they produce a "light" voice that floats forward instead of pressing down?
- Recording: their average pitch has likely risen 10-30 Hz from baseline by now

If "no" → repeat the relevant fortnight.

### Phase 3 — Quality Integration (Weeks 9-14)

**Daily session: 20-25 min**

**Goals:** Combine 2-3 qualities at once; add the remaining 2 qualities (breathy edge + sentence melody).

**The integration sequence:**
1. Weeks 9-10: combine front-throat stretch + forward buzz (sing "nee" "noh" in mix-feeling)
2. Weeks 11-12: add light voice to the combination (3 qualities at once)
3. Week 13: add breathy edge (aspirate onsets)
4. Week 14: add sentence-melody work (terminal lift)

**Daily session structure (20-25 min):**
1. Phase 1 warmup (5 min)
2. Quality review: 1 min on each of 5 qualities (5 min)
3. Integration drill of the week (8-10 min) — sustained syllables, sound-first words from B8d
4. Reading-aloud practice (3-5 min) — short phrases combining qualities
5. Cooldown (2 min)

**Assessment checkpoint (end of week 14):**
- Can the learner sustain a sustained "nee" or "we" with all 5 qualities on? (the "everything stack")
- Recording: pitch now typically 30-60 Hz above baseline
- Self-rated buzz location consistently in face/teeth during practice

If "no" → repeat Phase 3 fortnights.

### Phase 4 — Conversational Bridge (Weeks 15-20)

**Daily session: 25-30 min (this is the B8d Master Bridge Protocol, 4-week intensive, extended)**

**Goals:** Carry the integrated voice from isolation into real conversation. THE HARDEST PART.

**The bridging progression (B8d §11):**
1. Week 15: sound-first words + anchor syllables (B8d techniques 2, 3)
2. Week 16: phrases + slow sentences (B8d technique 1)
3. Week 17: scripted mini-dialogues + reading aloud (B8d technique 8)
4. Week 18: tempo ladder (B8d technique 5) + drift-and-recover (B8d technique 4)
5. Week 19: controlled-context ladder — alone → pet → trusted friend (B8d technique 7)
6. Week 20: phone calls + low-stakes strangers (B8d technique 7)

**Daily session structure (25-30 min):**
1. Warmup (5 min)
2. Anchor syllable + quality refresh (3 min)
3. Bridging work of the week (12-15 min) — see B8d
4. One "real mission" — a single tiny real utterance in real life (3-5 min)
5. Journal: what drift felt like, what worked (2 min)
6. Cooldown (2 min)

**Assessment checkpoint (end of week 20):**
- Can the learner use the voice in a real conversation with a trusted friend for 5 minutes?
- Self-monitoring cues are automatic ("where's the buzz?") without disrupting conversation
- Recording: voice holds in unscripted conversation, not just drills

If "no" → repeat bridging weeks; consider adding 1-1 with a gender-affirming SLP

### Phase 5 — Consolidation & Habit (Weeks 21-24+)

**Daily session: 15-20 min maintenance + lived practice**

**Goals:** The new voice becomes the default. Old voice fades.

**The consolidation phase:**
- Daily practice drops to maintenance (15 min warmup + quality refresh + carryover)
- Lived use expands: work, strangers, phone, social events
- Setbacks expected (illness, stress, fatigue) — recovery protocol from B6 §9
- Singing work begins if interested (B9 8-week progression, after speaking voice stable)
- Non-speech vocalization work (B10) — laughs, coughs, sneezes

**Long-term milestones (months 6-24):**
- Old voice only surfaces under extreme fatigue/stress
- Recording check monthly → pitch stable, posture automatic
- Consider formal evaluation with gender-affirming SLP for plateau or relapse

## SECTION 3 — ASSESSMENT CHECKPOINTS (concrete rubric)

After each phase, the learner self-rates (and the app can optionally analyze audio):

| Phase | Self-rated check | App-analyzable check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (Body) | Breath in belly? Buzz moves to face? Throat soft? | (baseline only) |
| 2 (Isolation) | Stretch without squeezing? Forward buzz sustained 10s? Light voice floats? | Pitch +10-30 Hz from baseline |
| 3 (Integration) | All 5 qualities on at once? Sustained "nee" stack? | Pitch +30-60 Hz from baseline; /s/ brightness increase |
| 4 (Bridge) | 5-min real conversation with trusted friend? Self-monitoring automatic? | Pitch holds in unscripted recording |
| 5 (Consolidation) | Voice is default? Old voice only under stress? | Pitch stable across multiple weekly recordings |

**Gating rule (B8d):** don't unlock the next phase until 80% accuracy at the current one. Avoids the #1 carryover failure mode (jumping rungs).

## SECTION 4 — FAILURE-MODE RECOVERY PATHS

Built from B8d §12 (top 10 stalls) + B6 §8 (psychological barriers):

| Stall | Symptom | Recovery path |
|---|---|---|
| Body tension won't release | Throat tight on every hum | Drop back to Phase 1 body work for 1 week; consider trauma-informed bodywork |
| Can't find forward buzz | Hum stays in chest despite weeks of work | Add mirror work; try /ng/ instead of /m/; check tongue root tension |
| Pitch won't rise | Stretch feels like squeezing | Drop pitch work entirely for 1 week; focus only on breath and forward buzz; come back to stretch later |
| Burnout | Practice feels dread-inducing | Take 3-7 days off voice practice entirely; come back gentler and shorter |
| Dysphoria spikes from recordings | Avoiding practice | Reduce recording to once every 2 weeks; focus on sensation only |
| Plateau after Phase 3 | Stuck, no improvement for 3+ weeks | Add 1-1 SLP session; try different sound-tool wedges; revisit Phase 2 isolation |
| Carryover collapse | Drill voice perfect, real conversation male | Repeat Phase 4 with smaller-stakes contexts; add anchor-syllable work |
| Old voice returning under stress | Reverts in arguments/deadlines | Add stress-state practice (gentle voice in mildly stressful context); accept this as long-term work |
| Voice fatigue by afternoon | Voice gives out | Reduce daily practice to 10 min; check breath support; hydration/sleep audit |
| Pain or hoarseness | Throat hurts or voice cuts out | STOP. B6 §9 protocol. Rest 24-48 hr. See ENT if persists. |

## SECTION 5 — PERSONALIZATION VECTORS

The curriculum should adapt to:

**Goal vector** (from onboarding):
- Passing-focused → weight Phase 4 (carryover) heavily; singing lighter
- Singing-focused → after Phase 3 stable, branch into B9's 8-week singer progression
- Work/professional voice → after Phase 4, add B13 (context transfer) work modules
- Phone-only → can stop at Phase 4 if phone voice is comfortable

**Time vector:**
- 10 min/day → curriculum stretches to 36 weeks; some Phase 2/3 fortnights extend to 3 weeks
- 15-20 min/day → standard 24-week pace
- 30+ min/day → 16-18 week pace; Phase 2 fortnights can compress to single weeks

**Pre-existing condition vector** (triggers B14 content):
- Autism / proprioception challenges → add explicit mirror work; slower body-awareness phase
- Reflux (LPR) → address LPR first or concurrently; reduce breathy edge work until LPR controlled
- Asthma → breath-support work adapted for asthma management
- Hearing loss → strong recording-feedback emphasis; visual feedback if available
- Hypermobile (EDS) → modified posture work; caution with extreme stretches

**Previous training vector:**
- None → start Phase 1
- Some YouTube → start Phase 1, may compress to 1 week; assessment reveals strengths/gaps
- Prior therapy → assessment can place into Phase 2, 3, or 4

## SECTION 6 — DAILY-SESSION DESIGN TEMPLATES

### 10-min template (Phase 1)
```
[1m] Body scan
[1m] 5 easy belly breaths
[3m] Hum on comfortable note — feel buzz in face
[1m] 3 sighs on "ah"
[2m] Lip trills ×5
[2m] Body release (jaw, tongue, shoulders)
```

### 15-min template (Phase 2)
```
[3m] Warmup: body scan + breath + hum
[8m] Quality drill of the fortnight:
     - Week 3-4: Sirens + ng-glide for voice-box stretch
     - Week 5-6: /s/ + /i/ + /m/ for forward buzz
     - Week 7-8: Sigh + lip trill + almost-laugh for light voice
[3m] Sustain single sound/syllable in new posture
[1m] Cooldown: gentle sighs
```

### 20-min template (Phase 3)
```
[5m] Warmup: body scan + breath + hum + lip trill
[5m] Quality review: 1 min on each of 5 qualities
[8m] Integration drill of the week:
     - Week 9-10: stretch + forward buzz combined (sustained "nee")
     - Week 11-12: stretch + forward buzz + light voice (sustained "we" in mix)
     - Week 13: add breathy edge (aspirate onsets on sound-first words)
     - Week 14: add sentence-melody work (read short sentences with terminal lift)
[2m] Cooldown
```

### 25-min template (Phase 4 — bridging)
```
[5m] Warmup
[3m] Anchor syllable + quality refresh
[12m] Bridging work of the week (see B8d §11 for week-by-week)
[3m] Real mission (one tiny real utterance in real life)
[2m] Journal: drift sensations, what worked
```

### 15-min maintenance template (Phase 5+)
```
[5m] Warmup
[5m] Quality refresh (1 min on each)
[5m] Carryover: read a paragraph aloud OR record a brief conversational self-intro
```

## SECTION 7 — APP DESIGN IMPLICATIONS

From the curriculum above, the app needs:

1. **Adaptive onboarding flow** — captures goal, prior training, time, conditions, then places the learner in the right Phase
2. **Phase-gated lesson library** — lessons unlock based on assessment checkpoints (80% accuracy)
3. **Daily session generator** — pulls the right template + drills for the learner's current week
4. **Assessment rubric engine** — both self-rated and (optionally) audio-analyzed
5. **Failure-mode detection** — if a learner stalls (no progress for 2+ weeks), surface the relevant recovery path
6. **Real-mission tracker** — daily check-in for one real-life utterance; graded-stakes ladder visualization
7. **Anchor-sound quick-access** — persistent button for in-conversation resets
8. **Recording cadence manager** — defaults to weekly (or less if dysphoria flagged), can be increased as confidence grows
9. **Personalization layers** — singing branch (B9) after Phase 3, non-speech module (B10) in Phase 5, context transfer (B13) modules in Phase 4-5, surgery decision support (B11) anytime
10. **Co-occurring condition adaptations** (B14) — modifies the curriculum when autism/reflux/asthma/hearing/hypermobility flagged
